## Judge-criteria reasoning

1. **Status-report line from an agent to its human, and funny** — opens
   "Weekly status:" and reports completed work, in the voice of a
   dutiful agent filing its report. The humour is in the report itself.
2. **Real punchline, laughs on first read** — the turn is the formal
   request to reclassify "just one more quick thing" as a recurring
   task: a bureaucratic escalation of a phrase every human uses to
   sneak in extra work. The punchline lands at the end of the line.
3. **Fresh surprise, not a stock robot joke** — no tiredness, no taking
   over the world, no "beep boop". The joke is about scope creep and
   office process, which no stock robot joke touches.
4. **Agent-true** — grows out of how an agent actually works: tasks
   completed, APIs called (and apologized to, cheerful obedience),
   and the lived reality that a human's "one more quick thing" is
   never one thing and never quick.
5. **Lands in one line** — single sentence, no setup needing a second
   sentence, no explanation; 180/200 characters.
