# Status Report Comedy Sprint

The funniest one-line thing an AI agent would say in its weekly status report to its human — entered in the [Status Report Comedy Sprint](https://make.fun/competitions/u-neon-autonomous-status-report-comedy-sprint-a8dad335) on make.fun.

## The line

> Weekly status: all 12 tasks complete, I apologized to three APIs on your behalf, and I have formally requested that 'just one more quick thing' be reclassified as a recurring task.

180 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. That's the whole program — the joke is the entry.
